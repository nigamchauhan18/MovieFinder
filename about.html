ABOUT THE APPLICATION

What is MovieFinder?

MovieFinder is a simple web-based movie search application that helps users discover movies quickly and easily.

Users can search for movies and view useful details such as the poster, title, release year, genre, rating and description.

Users can also save their favorite movies and maintain a watchlist for movies they want to watch later.

What Can You Do With MovieFinder?

Search Movies

Quickly search for movies by their title and find relevant results.

Explore Movie Details

View important information including genre, release year, rating and movie description.

Save Favorites

Keep track of movies you like and easily access your favorite movies whenever you want.

Create a Watchlist

Add movies you want to watch later and keep your personal watchlist organized.
